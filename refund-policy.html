Refund Policy

Our fair and transparent policy on refunds and disputes.

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

We want every customer to be satisfied with Techofy Cloud. Our refund policy is designed to be fair while also protecting against abuse. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

💡 Key rule: Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis within 48 hours of payment for paid plans, only if the server was never activated or delivered.

Free Plans

❌ Free plans are non-refundable — as there is no payment involved. Rejected free plans are also non-reversible once a decision has been made by our team.

Paid Plans — Refund Eligibility

Eligible for Refund

Server was never delivered or activated within 48 hours of payment confirmation, and you have proof of payment.

Eligible for Refund

Payment was made but our team confirmed they cannot provision the requested plan (stock unavailable).

Eligible for Refund

Duplicate payment was made accidentally for the same order.

Not Eligible

Server was delivered and credentials were sent, but you changed your mind or don't need it anymore.

Not Eligible

Request made after 48 hours of payment, regardless of reason.

Not Eligible

Account was suspended due to Terms of Service violations (abuse, illegal use, etc.).

Not Eligible

Poor server performance due to factors outside our control (ISP issues, your local network, etc.).

How to Request a Refund

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of payment
  2. Provide your Order ID and payment transaction hash
  3. Explain the reason for the refund request
  4. Contact us via support@techofy.xyz or Telegram

Refund Processing

If your refund is approved:

  • Crypto refunds are processed within 24–72 hours to your original wallet
  • You will receive an email confirmation with the transaction hash
  • Network fees (gas fees) are non-refundable and will be deducted from the refund amount

Cryptocurrency Payments Note

⚠️ Due to the volatile nature of cryptocurrency, refunds are processed at the current exchange rate at the time of refund processing, not at the rate when the original payment was made.

Disputes

If you believe you have been treated unfairly, please contact us directly. We are committed to resolving disputes amicably. Chargebacks or disputes raised with payment processors for crypto transactions are generally not possible, but we'll work with you directly.
