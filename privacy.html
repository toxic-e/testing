Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how we handle your data.

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Techofy Cloud:

  • Account data: First name, last name, email address
  • Order data: Plan type, order ID, payment status
  • Technical data: IP address, browser fingerprint (for fraud prevention), user agent
  • Communication data: Messages you send to support
  • Proof images: YouTube subscription screenshots (for free plan verification only)

2. Why We Collect This Data

  • To process and deliver your server orders
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud
  • To enforce our one-free-plan-per-device policy
  • To send order confirmations and server credentials
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our services

3. Device Fingerprinting

🔍 We use browser/device fingerprinting exclusively to prevent abuse of our free plan. This fingerprint helps us identify if the same device is trying to claim multiple free servers, even across different email accounts.

This data is hashed and stored securely. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

4. IP Address Logging

We log IP addresses for security purposes, abuse prevention, and to ensure our free plan is fairly distributed. IP addresses are stored for up to 90 days and then purged.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored in secure databases. Server credentials sent via email are not stored on our servers after delivery. We use encrypted connections for all data transmission.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • When required by law or valid legal process
  • With our email service provider solely for sending transactional emails
  • With our hosting infrastructure providers for service delivery

7. Screenshots

YouTube subscription screenshots submitted for free plan verification are stored temporarily for review purposes only. They are deleted after verification (within 7 days) and are never shared publicly.

8. Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Remember if you've claimed a free server (fraud prevention)
  • Store your cookies for security checks

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

9. Data Retention

  • Order records: retained for 1 year for support purposes
  • IP logs: 90 days
  • Screenshots: 7 days after order review
  • Email addresses: retained while your account is active

10. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us at support@techofy.xyz with your request.

11. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at anyone under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

14. Contact

Privacy questions? Reach us at support@techofy.xyz or Telegram.
